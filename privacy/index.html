Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Stratford Advertisements ("Stratford," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly - such as your name, business name, email, phone number, website, and details about your business - when you contact us, complete our onboarding form, or purchase a subscription. We also collect limited technical data (such as IP address and general usage) through standard web tools.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and manage our advertising services
  • To communicate with you about your account and campaigns
  • To process payments through our payment provider
  • To improve our services

Advertising & Third-Party Platforms

We run advertising campaigns on Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram) on behalf of clients. When we manage your account, you retain ownership of your ad account and data. We may use tools such as the Meta Pixel to measure ad performance. These platforms process data under their own privacy policies.

Payments

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We do not store your full payment card details; Stripe handles that information under its own privacy and security standards.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate (such as our payment processor and advertising platforms), or where required by law.

Data Retention & Security

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal obligations, and we use reasonable measures to protect it.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. You may opt out of marketing emails at any time.
