The Opportunity

White Rocks carries something almost no development ever has: Universal Music Group's first U.S. hotel and private residences, set in the Austin Hill Country alongside a five-star hotel and a 10,000-seat venue. That brand is a magnet for exactly the buyer you want.

Our role is focused and clear — put that story in front of the people who can afford to be part of it, and capture their interest early. We do not replace your brokerage or brand team. We feed them a steady, qualified pipeline, so when sales opens, your team opens to a warm list rather than a cold start.

Reaching the Right Buyer

For a product at this price, the goal is not volume — it is qualified interest. Meta is not where a $2M residence is purchased; it is where the right buyer raises their hand. We then hand that interest to your team to qualify and close in person.

Retargeting

Quality Over Volume
A focused set of genuinely qualified registrants is worth more than thousands of unqualified clicks. We optimize for lead quality.
