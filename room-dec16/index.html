White Rocks Get-Together · December 16

Working the Room

Ethan + Preston. Curious, confident, memorable. Collect contacts, not deals.
🧠

The Mindset

  • You're not here to sell. You're here to listen, connect, be memorable.
  • The win is a FOLLOW-UP, not a signature.
  • You belong here - on the White Rocks marketing + Chris's grandson. Own it quietly.
  • Confidence = calm curiosity, not pitching. They smell try-hard instantly.
👋

The Opener

Say"Hi, I'm Ethan - I'm Chris Milam's grandson. What's your connection to White Rocks?"

Then LISTEN. Keep them talking about themselves:

  • "How'd you get into that?"
  • "What are you working on right now?"
  • "What's got you most excited this year?"
🎯

Your Line (when they ask what you do)

Say it calm + confident"I run a Meta ads agency - right now I'm working on the pre-launch marketing for White Rocks."

Then turn it back: "But I'd love to hear more about what you're working on." Mention it, don't pitch it. Let them lean in.

🧊

Preston's Line

Preston says"I run a 3D-printing company - Austin 3D Prints. We make custom parts and products, made right here in Austin."

If asked more: "We do custom and replacement parts - if someone needs a part they can't find, we print it. Just getting it off the ground, but it's moving." (Store is live: atxprints.com)

Preston does NOT claim White Rocks - that's Ethan's lane. His own builder story is a strength.

🤝

Hype Each Other

Ethan about Preston"Preston runs a 3D-print company that's taking off."
Preston about Ethan"Ethan runs an ads agency - he's doing the White Rocks pre-launch marketing."

A friend vouching for you beats bragging. Two sharp young builders backing each other = memorable.

📇

The Soft Close (get the follow-up)

Say"I'd love to tell you more when we're not in the middle of all this - could I grab your contact and follow up this week?"

That's the whole win. Get their contact + jot ONE thing you talked about. Never try to close in the room.

✅

Do / Don't

  • Ask more than you talk. Be genuinely interested.
  • Remember names, repeat them back.
  • Dress sharp. Firm handshake. Eye contact. Stand tall.
  • Split up to cover ground, regroup to compare notes.
  • Don't pitch hard or hand out "deals."
  • Don't lead with "my grandfather" / "Preston's dad" to impress.
  • Don't cluster together the whole time.
  • Don't try to close anyone in the room.
📨

The Follow-Up (where it becomes business)

Within 24-48h, for each contact:

  • "Great meeting you at [event] - really enjoyed hearing about [specific thing]. Would love to stay in touch."
  • NO pitch yet. Just continue the relationship.
  • Give value before you ask. Nurture over weeks.
  • Then, when natural: "I'd love to show you what I do sometime."
📋

Pre-Event Checklist

  • Dress sharp
  • Phone charged (save contacts) + notes ready
  • Know your one-liner cold
  • Goal: 3-5 real contacts + something to remember about each
  • Mindset: curious, calm, memorable - NOT selling
The One Thing

Walk in curious, not selling. Mention White Rocks, don't pitch it. Collect contacts, not deals. Then follow up and nurture - that's where a handshake becomes a client.
